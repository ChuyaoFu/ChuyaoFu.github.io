I am Chuyao Fu, a senior undergraduate student in Electronic Information Engineering at SUSTech. My research focuses on **world models for robot learning**, including representation learning, generative modeling, and the interaction between learned world models and robot control policies.

At SUSTech, I am advised by Prof. [**Qinghu Meng**](https://faculty.sustech.edu.cn/?tagid=mengqh&iscss=1&snapid=1&orderby=date&go=2&lang=en) and Prof. [**Hong Zhang**](https://faculty.sustech.edu.cn/?tagid=zhangh33&iscss=1&snapid=1&orderby=date&go=2). I also conduct research with the PKU HMI Lab under Prof. [**Shanghang Zhang**](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/info/1089/1747.htm). I am currently spending Fall 2026 at UC Berkeley.

My long-term goal is to build robots that understand physical dynamics and interactions, enabling them to generalize beyond imitation.
