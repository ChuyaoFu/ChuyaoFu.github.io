I am Chuyao Fu, an undergraduate student in Electronic Information Engineering at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). I build predictive representations and learning systems for reliable physical autonomy, with a focus on **world models**, **vision-language-action models**, and **robot learning**.

At SUSTech, I am advised by Prof. [**Qinghu Meng**](https://faculty.sustech.edu.cn/?tagid=mengqh&iscss=1&snapid=1&orderby=date&go=2&lang=en) and Prof. [**Hong Zhang**](https://faculty.sustech.edu.cn/?tagid=zhangh33&iscss=1&snapid=1&orderby=date&go=2). I also conduct research with the PKU HMI Lab under Prof. [**Shanghang Zhang**](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/info/1089/1747.htm). I am currently a research intern at Muka Robotics, working on world-model representation learning and foundation-model pretraining. One recent application is a physical-world-model system that enables a robot to play tabletop curling against a human.

My long-term goal is to connect perception, prediction, and action in systems that can adapt and operate over long horizons in the physical world.
