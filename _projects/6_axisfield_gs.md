## Overview

AxisField-GS studies articulation recovery from a single-state semantic 3D Gaussian
Splatting asset. The framework predicts dense Gaussian-level motion fields and uses
them to recover the rotation axes of revolute joints.

I co-led this project with Shengzhe Gan as an equal-contribution first author. The work
is under review at ACCV 2026.
