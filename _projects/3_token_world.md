## Overview

Token-World investigates whether an embodied world model can predict future observations directly in a compact visual-token space. The goal is to support action-conditioned simulation for VLA policies without requiring pixel-space reconstruction as an intermediate output.

I lead this first-author project and am responsible for the problem formulation, method
design, experiments, and manuscript preparation. This is active research; results and
technical details will be released after the experimental study is complete.
