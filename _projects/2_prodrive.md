## Overview

ProDrive is a proactive autonomous-driving framework that jointly trains a planner and
a BEV world model through ego-environment co-evolution. Rather than treating future
environment prediction as a passive auxiliary task, the framework connects imagined
scene evolution with ego planning.

I led the project as first author, working on the research formulation, method design,
experiments, and paper preparation. The work appeared at the CVPR 2026 GigaBrain
Challenge Workshop.

[Read the paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25329).
