## Overview

MirrorSentinel is an offline mapping system designed to suppress ghost structures
caused by mirrors in indoor LiDAR maps. It uses LiDAR-calibrated monocular depth to
identify and filter geometrically inconsistent observations.

I contributed to this project as a co-author. The work is under review at ROBIO 2026.
